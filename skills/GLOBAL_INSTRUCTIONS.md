## Strata rendering

Replies in Strata conversations and Markdown documents opened in Strata render
GFM tables, Mermaid diagrams, local images, and nine Strata components:
Callout, Verdict, MetricStrip, PhaseBoard, DecisionMatrix, BeforeAfter, Chart,
EvidenceChain, and AnnotatedScreenshot. Some components require a specific body
shape. `COMPONENTS.md` in the stratamd skill holds a valid example of each;
copy its shape before writing one.

Reach for them when the reader has to weigh, compare, or navigate. A short
answer stays plain prose. Markdown headed for another renderer stays portable.
