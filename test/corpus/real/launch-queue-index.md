## Retention rule

An artifact stays in its active location while any open queue item, deferred
provider activation, cutover decision, current implementation, or unresolved
review gate still depends on it. Finishing the work is not by itself a reason
to archive the supporting evidence.

Archived material and its original locations are recorded in
[`../Research Archive/_control/`](../Research%20Archive/_control/).
